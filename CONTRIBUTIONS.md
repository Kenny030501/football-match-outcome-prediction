## Portfolio reconstruction

The public portfolio edition was prepared and is maintained by Hongru Da from
the team's original research design. Its repository-level contributions are:

- documenting the same-match feature leakage in the archived analysis;
- separating the original course result from a valid pre-match forecast;
- building a strict downloader, source manifest, row quarantine, and canonical
  point-in-time feature pipeline;
- auditing normalized market-average closing probabilities over four expanding
  out-of-time test seasons;
- comparing raw market probabilities with recalibration and a public-feature
  augmentation under pre-specified decision rules;
- reporting clustered-bootstrap uncertainty, calibration, drift, and subgroup
  results;
- documenting limitations, source quality, and reuse boundaries.

These statements describe the portfolio reconstruction. They do not represent
the original team project as the sole work of one contributor.
